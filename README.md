## Archive entries

These are the engagement types that do not have a case study elsewhere.

### CRM lead-capture and workflow automation

- **Project type:** Recurring client engagements
- **Service:** Lead capture, pipeline and follow-up automation inside a hosted marketing CRM, including AI-assisted response steps
- **Tools:** CRM platform automation builder, web forms, email and SMS steps, calendar booking
- **Deliverable:** Configured lead-capture and follow-up workflows, handed over working
- **Status:** Completed
- **Evidence:** 19 completed Fiverr orders, 8 carrying buyer ratings
- **Privacy note:** Client accounts, pipeline contents, contact data and campaign copy are not published.

### Property and listings administration

- **Project type:** Short client engagements
- **Service:** Property listing preparation, listing data entry, email handling and comparison research
- **Tools:** Spreadsheets, email, listing platforms
- **Deliverable:** Prepared listings and organised supporting records
- **Status:** Completed
- **Evidence:** 2 completed Fiverr orders, 1 carrying a buyer rating
- **Privacy note:** Property addresses, owner details and client records are not published.

### Document formatting and office files

### Keyword and competitor research

## What this archive does not contain

No buyer names or usernames, email addresses, order identifiers, payment details, credentials, private file names, private URLs, or client data of any kind. No screenshots of client accounts.

Where a service line's implementation detail is not published, it is because the client did not agree to it being published, not because the work is unavailable.

## Accounting note

Orders are counted, not estimated. The 118 completed orders not itemised here were not reconstructed, and nothing has been invented to make the totals agree. If the remaining history is re-read later, this archive will be extended from the platform record rather than from memory.
